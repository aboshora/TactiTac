TactiTac

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Overview

TactiTac is an offline Tic Tac Toe game published by CodePoint Technology. This policy explains how TactiTac handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

TactiTac does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or messages.

Game data and storage

Gameplay, scores, and settings are used locally on your device to provide the game experience. This information is not transmitted to our servers by TactiTac.

Advertising and third parties

TactiTac does not contain third-party advertising, analytics tracking, or in-app purchases. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information.

Children’s privacy

TactiTac does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not collect personal information, no account or identity information is required to play.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s data practices change. Any updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact CodePoint Technology through the public TactiTac GitHub Issues page.
